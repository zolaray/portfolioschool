About Us

Lycée 1er Novembre 1954 is a leading secondary education institution named after the historic date of the Algerian Revolution, symbolizing a bridge between national heritage and future innovation. We are committed to academic excellence and the development of responsible citizens prepared for the challenges of tomorrow.

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster academic excellence while nurturing civic responsibility. We provide a competitive environment for students in Science, Mathematics, and Literature, preparing them for the National Baccalaureate with rigorous standards.

At Lycée 1er Novembre 1954, we believe in developing well-rounded individuals who are not only intellectually prepared but also deeply connected to their cultural heritage and social responsibilities.

Special Features

What sets us apart as an institution of learning

💻 Digital Integration

Advanced use of digital platforms (Parent Portal) for real-time tracking of student progress and grades. We embrace technology to enhance communication and transparency.
